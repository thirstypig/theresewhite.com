**TAKE #1 -- PROBLEM:** A problem employee just received their second written warning. HR is following progressive discipline to the letter. Meanwhile, the employee is still making everyone miserable; customers are complaining, and the company’s best are job-hunting.

**TAKE #2 -- PATTERN:** Progressive discipline teaches employees exactly how badly they can behave . . . *without getting fired*. It's a roadmap for mediocrity, not a path to improvement. While you're checking the boxes, they're learning the behavior boundaries. Yes, your process may protect the company legally, but it may also be ruining it culturally.

**TAKE #3 -- PRACTICE:** Replace progressive discipline with *progressive support*. Instead of warning them about what they're doing wrong, teach them how to do things right. Invest in coaching, training, and very clear expectations. If there’s still a problem, then do what you must, but do it quickly and kindly.

**KEEP IN MIND:** Progressive discipline protects you from lawsuits, but not from losing your company’s soul.
