The Workplace Conflict Cost Calculator

Quantify the hidden operational tax of unresolved conflict between hard-to-replace contributors

The Problem

When two key team members reach an impasse, the financial drain rarely shows up as a line item. It quietly accumulates through delayed decisions, stalled initiatives, team hesitation, and senior leadership diverted from strategic priorities.

What This Tool Does

It delivers a clear, order-of-magnitude estimate of your costs to date and projects your exposure over the next 12 months if the situation remains unresolved.

Fast and Intuitive to Use

In under two minutes, a few straightforward observations about time and compensation become a clear financial snapshot of the situation. Rough numbers are fine. The point isn’t accounting precision; it’s order of magnitude—and that’s usually where the surprise lies.

How to use it. Work through sections A to C. Type exact figures for salaries and costs, and use the sliders for hours, which are estimates anyway. The results on the right update as you go. The starting values are examples; replace them with your own.
